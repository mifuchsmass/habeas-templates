================================================================================
           WORD TEMPLATE AUTOMATION GUIDE FOR HABEAS PLEADINGS
================================================================================

This guide explains how to format Microsoft Word (.docx) templates so our
habeas petition generator can automatically insert petitioner details, 
court headers, pronoun variations, custom case facts, and conditional paragraphs.


1. HOW PLACEHOLDERS WORK (FILLING IN BLANKS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Whenever you want the generator to fill in information from the form, wrap the
field name in square brackets: [Field Name]

A. Standard Primary Fields:
• [Petitioner Name]           -> Combines First, Middle, Last, and Suffix
                                 (e.g., "Jane M. Doe, Jr.")
• [Petitioner First Name]     -> "Jane"
• [Petitioner Middle Name]    -> "M."
• [Petitioner Last Name]      -> "Doe"
• [Petitioner Suffix]         -> "Jr."
• [Defendant Name] / [Respondent Name]
• [Case Number] / [Docket Number]
• [Jurisdiction]
• [Detention Facility] / [Facility]
• [Type of Habeas Petition]   -> Short code (e.g., "GO Class" or "Non-GO")
• [Type of Habeas Petition Full] -> Full text from dropdown

B. Custom Field Auto-Discovery (Tier 2 Fields):
You are NOT limited to the primary fields above! If your pleading needs specific 
factual details, simply type any bracketed tag in your Word template, such as:
• [Alien Registration Number]
• [Date of Entry]
• [Arresting Agency]
• [Prior Removal Order Date]

When you or a volunteer load the template into the generator, the engine 
AUTOMATICALLY DISCOVERS these tags and creates form input fields for them.

C. Key Formatting Features:
• Smart Caps:
  - If you type the placeholder in ALL CAPS: [PETITIONER NAME]
    -> Outputs in uppercase: JANE M. DOE
  - If you type the placeholder normally: [Petitioner Name]
    -> Outputs as entered: Jane M. Doe
• Missing Data Safeguard: If a user leaves a form field blank, the document 
  prints "*** MISSING DATA ***" in that spot so incomplete pleadings are never 
  accidentally filed.
• Curly Quotes: Word automatically turns straight quotes into curly "smart quotes." 
  The engine handles both straight and curly quotes automatically.


2. AUTOMATIC PRONOUN & GENDER TOGGLING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The intake form includes a 1-click pronoun selector:
  • He / Him           (Default Male)
  • She / Her          (Female)
  • They / Them        (Gender-Neutral / Non-Binary)
  • Petitioner         (Noun Only / Formal Pleading)

A. Output Reference Matrix

| Tag Typed in Word       | He / Him (Default) | She / Her | They / Them | Petitioner (Noun) |
| :---                    | :---               | :---      | :---        | :---              |
| [he/she] or [he]        | he                 | she       | they        | Petitioner        |
| [He/She] or [He]        | He                 | She       | They        | Petitioner        |
| [HE/SHE] or [HE]        | HE                 | SHE       | THEY        | PETITIONER        |
| [him/her] or [him]      | him                | her       | them        | Petitioner        |
| [Him/Her] or [Him]      | Him                | Her       | Them        | Petitioner        |
| [HIM/HER] or [HIM]      | HIM                | HER       | THEM        | PETITIONER        |
| [his/her] or [his]      | his                | her       | their       | Petitioner's      |
| [His/Her] or [His]      | His                | Her       | Their       | Petitioner's      |
| [HIS/HER] or [HIS]      | HIS                | HER       | THEIR       | PETITIONER'S      |
| [hers] or [his/hers]    | his                | hers      | theirs      | Petitioner's      |
| [himself/herself]       | himself            | herself   | themselves  | Petitioner        |
| [Petitioner]            | Petitioner         | Petitioner| Petitioner  | Petitioner        |
| [Petitioner's]          | Petitioner's       | Petitioner's| Petitioner's| Petitioner's    |

B. Verb & Grammatical Agreement Tags
When "They / Them" is selected, standard English grammar uses plural verb forms 
("they are", "they were", "they seek"). The engine provides built-in agreement tags:

| Agreement Tag Typed     | He / Him           | She / Her | They / Them | Petitioner        |
| :---                    | :---               | :---      | :---        | :---              |
| [is/are]                | is                 | is        | **are**     | is                |
| [was/were]              | was                | was       | **were**    | was               |
| [has/have]              | has                | has       | **have**    | has               |
| [brings/bring]          | brings             | brings    | **bring**   | brings            |
| [seeks/seek]            | seeks              | seeks     | **seek**    | seeks             |
| [contends/contend]      | contends           | contends  | **contend** | contends          |
| [s] (3rd-person suffix) | s                  | s         | *(empty)*   | s                 |
| [an individual/individuals] | an individual  | an individual | an individual | an individual |

C. The 2 Golden Rules for Drafting with Verbs:
• RULE 1: When the subject is a named party or title ("Petitioner [Petitioner Name]"):
  Named parties are ALWAYS singular nouns in legal pleadings. Hardcode singular verbs:
    ❌ INCORRECT: Petitioner [Petitioner Name] [is/are] detained...
    ✅ CORRECT:   Petitioner [Petitioner Name] is detained in [Detention Facility].

• RULE 2: When the subject is the Pronoun Placeholder (`[He/She]` or `[He]`):
  Use the verb agreement tags directly after pronoun placeholders:
    ✅ CORRECT:   [He/She] [is/are] currently detained in administrative custody.
    ✅ CORRECT:   [He/She] [seeks/seek] emergency injunctive relief.
    ✅ CORRECT:   [He/She] remain[s] in custody without statutory authority.

D. Conditional Pronoun Blocks:
You can also write condition blocks based on the selected pronoun option:
  [IF Pronouns = "nonbinary"]
  Petitioner uses gender-neutral they/them pronouns.
  [ELSE IF Pronouns = "petitioner"]
  Petitioner is referred to exclusively by title as "Petitioner."
  [END IF]


3. CONDITIONAL RULES (CHOOSING WHICH LANGUAGE TO INCLUDE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
You can show or hide paragraphs based on jurisdiction, case type, facility, or facts.

A. Single Condition ([IF] ... [END IF])
  [IF Jurisdiction = "Massachusetts"]
  See Mass. Gen. Laws ch. 260, § 2.
  [END IF]

B. Two Options ([IF] ... [ELSE] ... [END IF])
  [IF Type of Habeas Petition = "GO Class"]
  Petitioner brings this action on behalf of all similarly situated individuals.
  [ELSE]
  Petitioner brings this action individually.
  [END IF]

C. Multiple Branches ([IF] ... [ELSE IF] ... [ELSE] ... [END IF])
  [IF Jurisdiction = "Massachusetts"]
  UNITED STATES DISTRICT COURT FOR THE DISTRICT OF MASSACHUSETTS
  [ELSE IF Jurisdiction = "Rhode Island"]
  UNITED STATES DISTRICT COURT FOR THE DISTRICT OF RHODE ISLAND
  [ELSE]
  UNITED STATES DISTRICT COURT FOR THE DISTRICT OF [JURISDICTION]
  [END IF]


4. COMPARISONS & LOGICAL OPERATORS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
You can combine multiple criteria:
• Equals:                   [IF Jurisdiction = "Massachusetts"]
• Does NOT equal:           [IF Jurisdiction != "Massachusetts"]
• Both must be true (AND):  [IF Jurisdiction = "Massachusetts" and Petition Type = "GO Class"]
• Either can be true (OR):  [IF Jurisdiction = "Massachusetts" or Jurisdiction = "Rhode Island"]
• Numeric Comparisons:      [IF Days In Custody >= 180]

Note: Dropdown values can be matched either by short value ("Plymouth") or by full 
dropdown label ("Plymouth (Plymouth County Correctional Facility)").


5. AVOIDING BLANK LINES & KEEPING AUTO-NUMBERING INTACT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
RULE 1: Changing text INSIDE a numbered paragraph
Keep all conditional tags on the SAME line as the paragraph text:
  3. This action is brought pursuant to [Jurisdiction] law. [IF Jurisdiction = "Massachusetts"]See Mass. Gen. Laws ch. 260, § 2.[ELSE]See applicable statutory provisions.[END IF]

RULE 2: Making an ENTIRE numbered paragraph optional
Put [IF] and [END IF] on their OWN unnumbered lines before and after the paragraph:
  [IF Type of Habeas Petition = "GO Class"]
  4. Petitioner brings this class action pursuant to Fed. R. Civ. P. 23(b)(2).
  [END IF]

  When "Non-GO" is selected, Paragraph 4 disappears entirely, and subsequent 
  paragraphs automatically re-number consecutively with no blank gaps.


6. NESTING CONDITIONS (CONDITIONS INSIDE CONDITIONS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
You can nest conditional blocks inside other conditional blocks. Ensure every [IF] 
has a matching [END IF]:

  [IF Jurisdiction = "Massachusetts"]
    [IF Type of Habeas Petition = "GO Class"]
      Massachusetts GO Class Claims
    [ELSE]
      Massachusetts Individual Claims
    [END IF]
  [ELSE]
    Out-of-District Claims
  [END IF]


7. VALIDATING YOUR TEMPLATES (THE DRAFTING STUDIO)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Before distributing new templates to attorneys, always test them in the 
Volunteer Drafting Studio (`studio.html`):

1. Open `studio.html` in your browser.
2. Drag and drop your `.docx` file into the upload zone (or paste a paragraph snippet).
3. The Logic Inspector will immediately check for:
   • Unclosed `[IF]` or `[ELSE IF]` tags
   • Orphaned `[ELSE]` or `[END IF]` tags
   • Out-of-order tags (`[ELSE IF]` placed after `[ELSE]`)
   • Unbalanced quotation marks
4. If errors are found, the studio highlights the exact paragraph snippet and gives 
   step-by-step instructions on how to fix it in Word using Ctrl+F.
5. Use the Live Test Sandbox to simulate different form inputs and view a live 
   rendered preview or compiled `.docx` download.
